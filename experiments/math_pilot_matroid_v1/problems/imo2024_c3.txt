Let n be a positive integer. Seat 2n people around a circular table and designate
n disjoint pairs, so each person has exactly one partner.

Partners may shake hands whenever they occupy neighboring seats. A move exchanges
the occupants of two neighboring seats, including the two seats across the end of
any linear numbering of the circle.

Find M(n), the smallest nonnegative integer such that every initial seating can
be handled using at most M(n) moves, with every pair shaking hands at some point.
Handshakes can occur initially or between moves and require no move themselves.
Different pairs may shake hands at different times; they need not all be adjacent
at the end. Give a complete proof for every positive integer n.
